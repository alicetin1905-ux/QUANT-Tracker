    About Quant

    Quant builds Overledger, software that lets banks and enterprises connect different blockchains and existing payment systems without picking one network. Its customers are regulated institutions, and its recent wins are in tokenized bank money.

    QNT is the utility token: organizations pay for Overledger licences and network access in QNT. Supply is fixed at 14,612,493 tokens.

    Founded
    2015, London, by Gilbert Verdian (ex-Mastercard security lead, initiated ISO/TC 307 for blockchain standards)
    Token
    ERC-20 on Ethereum; bridged version on Solana since Oct 2026
    Product
    Overledger; Overledger Fusion (Jun 2025), a "Layer 2.5" multi-ledger rollup for cross-chain stablecoins and tokenized assets
    Tick size
    0.001 USD · 0.001 QNT on Crypto.com

    Track record

    • Project Rosalind (2023) with the BIS Innovation Hub and Bank of England, on APIs for retail CBDC
    • LACChain with the Inter-American Development Bank and Oracle, across 12 Latin American countries
    • Certified interoperability solution for Oracle Blockchain Platform
    • UK Finance GBTD tokenised deposits (live) and The Clearing House On-Chain Money (2027)

    Risks to watch

    • Price rose about 4x in a week on one announcement. Moves of 20% in a day have been common since.
    • Bank revenue does not have to flow through the token. How much demand the TCH deal creates for QNT is unclear.
    • New 50x perpetual futures add leverage that can cause sharp liquidation moves either way.
    • Crypto.com's QNT/USD book is thin (about $1M a day). Binance and other venues set the price.
